Chinese web fonts
=================
Headings: Adobe Source Han Serif CN Light (300).
Body: Adobe Source Han Sans CN Regular (400).

Official sources:
https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-serif
https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans

The local WOFF2 subsets are renamed DCCC Song and DCCC Hei, respectively.
Original font copyright and license records are retained. Their SIL OFL
licenses are included as source-han-serif-LICENSE.txt and
source-han-sans-LICENSE.txt.

Reproduce with scripts/subset-website-fonts.py using the official source
fonts in the ignored .cache/website-fonts/ directory. Full source fonts are
not shipped with this website.
