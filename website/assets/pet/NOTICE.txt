Colin27 anatomy
==============
Surface derived from the Brainstorm Colin27 2016 pial cortex:
https://neuroimage.usc.edu/brainstorm/Tutorials/DefaultAnatomy
Colin27 gray-matter mask distributed in the Cedalion Colin27 head model.

PET illustration
================
Derived cortical-MIP surface uptake values from 11 internal PET examples provided for this
DCCC website illustration. No native NIfTI images or original source case
identifiers are included in these assets. Supplied CL values are anchors,
not recomputed measurements. Visual interpolation between participants does
not model longitudinal progression or create clinical reference standards.
See manifest.json for processing details and limitations.
